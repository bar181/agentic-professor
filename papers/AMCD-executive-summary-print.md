## The Challenge

Course development is expensive and slow. Traditional approaches require 50-100 hours per module at costs of $5,000-20,000. Meanwhile, institutions face pressure to launch programs faster, scale offerings efficiently, and serve diverse audiences—all while maintaining quality.

## The AMCD Solution

The **Adaptive Modular Course Design (AMCD)** framework addresses these challenges by separating what must remain consistent from what should adapt.

**CORE** (Stable for All Learners)
- **C**aptivate: Hook with story, establish relevance
- **O**rient: Build systematic understanding
- **R**ealize: Apply through case study
- **E**xecute: Hands-on guided practice

**FLEX** (Adapts to Audience)
- Tiered problem sets matching learner level
- Industry-specific examples and cases
- Role-based scenarios (executive, practitioner, technical)

## Theoretical Foundation

AMCD synthesizes 12 peer-reviewed educational theories:

| Foundation | Application |
|------------|-------------|
| Bloom's Revised Taxonomy | Learning objective structure |
| Cognitive Load Theory | Managing mental effort |
| Zone of Proximal Development | Appropriate challenge levels |
| Differentiated Instruction | Meeting learners where they are |
| Narrative Pedagogy | Story as learning structure |
| Backward Design | Outcomes-first planning |

## Design Goals

| Goal | Mechanism |
|------|-----------|
| Structural consistency | 100% adherence to CORE format |
| Quality repeatability | Explicit rubrics and criteria |
| Audience adaptation | FLEX separation from CORE |
| Development efficiency | Templates eliminate decisions |

**Projected time reduction**: 30-45% (human-led AMCD adoption)

*Note: Projections require validation through pilot programs in your context.*

## What AMCD Provides

- Research-grounded 4-lesson module structure
- Clear voice guidelines for each lesson type
- Quality rubrics with explicit criteria
- FLEX system for audience adaptation
- Templates and documentation

## What AMCD Does Not Provide

- Guaranteed outcomes (no methodology can)
- Domain-specific content expertise
- Automated course generation
- Empirical validation data (requires further study)

## The Agentic Course Creator (ACC)

This repository includes exploratory AI agent specifications demonstrating how AMCD could be operationalized. The ACC is a **research companion and demonstration asset**—not a production tool or turnkey system.

## Who Should Consider AMCD

**Good fit:**
- Organizations needing consistent course structure
- Teams with instructional design capacity
- Domains with established knowledge bases
- Those willing to invest in methodology adoption

**Less suitable:**
- Highly specialized or proprietary content needs
- No instructional design expertise available
- Requirement for empirically validated outcomes now
