## Universities and Colleges

### Scenario 1: Online Degree Program Expansion

**Situation:**
A mid-sized university wants to launch an online bachelor's completion program. They have strong on-campus courses but limited online development capacity.

**Challenge:**
- 40 courses needed for the full program
- 3-year timeline to launch
- Budget cannot support 40x traditional development costs
- Faculty have classroom expertise but limited online design experience

**AMCD Application:**

| Approach | Traditional | With AMCD/ACC |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| Courses per year | 12-15 | 25-30 |
| Cost per course | $40,000-60,000 | $15,000-25,000 |
| Faculty time required | 80-120 hrs/course | 30-50 hrs/course |
| Consistency | Variable | High |

**Implementation:**
1. Train instructional design team on AMCD
2. Faculty provide source material and review output
3. Generate draft modules, faculty refine
4. ID team ensures quality and consistency

**Expected outcome:** Program launches on schedule with consistent pedagogical quality and reduced faculty burden.

### Scenario 3: Course Variant for Executive Education

**Situation:**
An existing MBA course needs to be adapted for an executive audience. Same content, different context and pacing.

**Challenge:**
- Executives have limited time (weekend intensive format)
- Need practical applications, not theoretical depth
- Must maintain academic rigor
- Original faculty not available for redevelopment

**AMCD Application:**

| Task | Time with ACC |
|------|---------------|
| Analyze original course | 2-3 hours |
| Configure for executive context | 1-2 hours |
| Generate adapted version | 1-2 hours |
| Review and refine | 8-12 hours |
| **Total** | **12-19 hours** |

Versus traditional adaptation: 60-100 hours

**Implementation:**
1. Input original course content as source material
2. Configure for workshop/executive context adaptation
3. Generate condensed, practice-focused version
4. Executive education director reviews and approves

**Expected outcome:** New revenue stream from existing IP with minimal additional development.

## Corporate Training

### Scenario 4: Enterprise-Wide Skill Development

**Situation:**
A Fortune 500 company needs to upskill 10,000 employees on data literacy. Training must work for functions from marketing to manufacturing.

**Challenge:**
- One curriculum, many audiences
- Must be relevant to each function's daily work
- Consistency required for organizational alignment
- Development time constrained by business need

**AMCD Application:**

| Audience | Traditional Approach | With AMCD |
|----------|---------------------|-----------|
| Base curriculum | Full development | Full development |
| Marketing variant | 80% redevelopment | 10% adaptation |
| Manufacturing variant | 80% redevelopment | 10% adaptation |
| Finance variant | 80% redevelopment | 10% adaptation |
| Per variant cost | $30,000-50,000 | $3,000-5,000 |

**Implementation:**
1. Develop master curriculum using AMCD structure
2. Create function-specific case studies and examples
3. Generate variants for each audience using adaptation agents
4. Function SMEs review their variants

**Expected outcome:** Consistent skill development across organization; rapid customization at fraction of traditional cost.

### Scenario 5: Compliance Training Modernization

**Situation:**
Annual compliance training is viewed as a burden. Completion rates are high (mandatory) but comprehension is low.

**Challenge:**
- Content is regulatory, not engaging
- Must meet legal requirements
- Learners complete as quickly as possible
- Actual behavior change is goal

**AMCD Application:**

| Traditional | AMCD Approach |
|-------------|---------------|
| Policy-focused | Scenario-focused (Lesson 3) |
| Generic examples | Role-specific cases |
| Check-the-box | Understanding verified |
| Annual rewrite | Efficient updates |

**Implementation:**
1. Map regulatory requirements to learning objectives
2. Generate scenario-based CORE lessons with real situations
3. Create tiered problem sets that test application, not recall
4. Compliance team reviews for accuracy

**Expected outcome:** Higher engagement, better comprehension verification, easier annual updates.

## Key Patterns Across Use Cases

### When AMCD Excels

| Pattern | Examples |
|---------|----------|
| Scale with consistency | Multi-course programs, enterprise training |
| Audience variants | Executive versions, function-specific training |
| Time pressure | Product launches, market windows |
| Quality standardization | Volunteer content, association programs |
| Efficient updates | Compliance, certification prep |

### When AMCD Requires Caution

| Pattern | Recommendation |
|---------|----------------|
| Cutting-edge research | Heavy expert review, may not be best fit |
| Highly regulated | Expert review essential, add compliance scoring |
| Unique pedagogical approach | AMCD structure may not fit |
| Very short content | May be overkill for micro-learning |
